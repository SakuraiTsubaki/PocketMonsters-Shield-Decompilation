# Exhaustive Public-Source Coverage Tracker

This document prevents a seed list of sources from being mistaken for a completed Shield survey.

The project has **no locally owned retail dump**. Japanese material is the comparison baseline, but completion requires independent enumeration of Shield-specific data, every regional/language surface, every update/DLC state, distributions, network data, and public technical research.

## Completion vocabulary

- **Not started** — family known, not yet enumerated.
- **Enumerating** — discovery/indexing underway.
- **Indexed** — known items have individual records but are not all reviewed.
- **Reviewed** — every indexed item has been read/classified and useful claims extracted.
- **Cross-checked** — reviewed claims compared against independent evidence where practical.
- **Exhausted** — repeated alternate-name/language/domain/archive searches found no additional material at the survey date.

## Secondary cross-check corpora

Bulbapedia, Serebii, Pokémon Database, Smogon/Pokémon Showdown, TCRF, attributable datamines, archived forum/research posts, old wiki revisions and web archives remain separate source families. None can silently replace primary provenance.

## Completion rule

Shield is not “done because Sword was surveyed.” Every shared source must be reclassified for `Shield / Sword / both / version-dependent`, and every Shield-only difference must have its own record.
